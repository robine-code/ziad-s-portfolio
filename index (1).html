Case study

1

Business problem

Sales figures alone don't explain performance. The goal was a single analytical solution showing how sales, profitability, customer behavior, returns, and operations connect, so trends and problem areas are visible in one place.

2

Data preparation

Power Query handled the cleaning and transformation of the source data, turning raw tables into consistent, analysis-ready inputs for the model.

3

Data modeling

The data was organized into a star schema: a central fact table linked to descriptive dimension tables. This keeps relationships clear, filtering predictable, and measures reliable.

4

Analysis

DAX measures calculate the core KPIs for sales, profit, returns, and shipping, while time intelligence supports analysis of performance across periods.

5

Dashboard

Four report pages (Overview, Sales & Profitability, Customer Analysis, and Operations) are linked with navigation buttons. Year, Region, and Category filters, KPI cards, and charts make it easy to explore, while drill-through, bookmarks, and tooltips add depth.

6

Insights

Together, the KPIs help users understand how profitable sales are, how many orders are returned, and how quickly orders ship, pointing to areas that may need attention.

What the dashboard surfaces

Examples of what a user can see at a glance in the report.

FurnitureProfit margin is far lower than in Technology and Office Supplies, even though sales are comparable.
Standard ClassThe slowest ship mode, averaging about 5 days, while Same Day is close to zero.
Consumer segmentAbout half of all 793 customers (51.58%), followed by Corporate and Home Office.
